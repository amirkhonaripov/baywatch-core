## 2. Data and splits (details in `ident_sources.md` and `ident_splits.md`)

* **OCR training**: AUTO.RIA crops KZ (8,145), KG (28,941) and RU (25,000 of 49,372) from the native train parts; 60,000 synthetic Uzbek crops; 4,492 crops cut from synthetic composites (section 3). Validation: native AUTO.RIA val parts + 3,000 synthetic.
* **OCR test sets** (never trained on): AUTO.RIA KZ 279, KG 1,068, RU 2,845 (native test parts); synthetic UZ 3,000 (own seed, texts disjoint from train); **real Uzbek 299 crops** (see section 5).
* **Detector training**: Roboflow Uzbek_Plates train split (2,304 images = one augmented copy per source photo), plus for the `full` variant 1,200 CCPD images (DB 480, Weather 300, Challenge 144, Base 276; train half by file-name hash), 700 RodoSol train-block images and 1,500 synthetic composites.
* **Detector test sets**: real UZ 301 images; RodoSol 1,000 (block-split test part, cars-br + cars-me only: the Kaggle mirror has no motorcycle subsets); CCPD DB 511, Weather 511, Challenge 597 (eval halves).
* No real Uzbek OCR text labels were used for training: the real Uzbek crops only enter the detector (boxes). The OCR sees Uzbek plates only as synthetic renders.
* Not obtainable / not used: Mendeley "Central Asia" Uzbek folder (plates blurred by the sellers; used as photo backgrounds only), Unidata sample (no Uzbek images), UFPR-VeSV, platesmania scrapes (not downloaded), VIN data (stretch goal not attempted).

## 3. Synthetic Uzbek plate generator (declared method)

`lanes/ident/synth/render.py` draws plates from the format rules (`uz_format.py`); `augment.py` degrades them; `composite.py` pastes them on real photos.

* **Layout**: 520 x 110 mm rim, region field (thin rectangle at the left; **green fill + white digits for EV**, an assumption from the research notes: "green region field"), six character slots on a fixed grid (1 letter + 3 digits + 2 letters, or 3 digits + 3 letters), flag + "UZ" block at the right. Motorcycle: square two-row plate (3 digits on top with the region at the upper right, 2 letters below); this layout is taken from Wikipedia/goldenpages text and **was never compared with a photo**.
* **Font**: DIN-like faces under SIL OFL 1.1 (Barlow, Barlow (Semi)Condensed, Saira Semi Condensed, Share Tech) with random weight/width jitter. No FE-Schrift digitisation with a verifiable free licence was found; glyph details such as the slotted zero are only approximated (the real-photo test shows the OCR still reads real plates).
* **Texts are invented**: region skewed towards real codes (01 about 18 %, the 14 base codes about 40 %, uniform 01-99 for the rest), random series letters (I rare), random digits. Test and train texts are disjoint.
* **Degradations** (all random, seeded): perspective/roll/yaw, resolution loss (64-400 px wide), day / overcast / dusk / night / night-IR / over-exposed scenarios, glare, shadow, dirt, rain streaks and droplets, partial occlusion, Gaussian/motion/defocus blur, sensor noise, JPEG, detector-box jitter and Roboflow-style stretch.
* **Composites** (detector data + OCR crops): the rendered plate is warped with the homography of the plate it replaces (RodoSol corners, CCPD vertices, or the box of the fast-alpr detector on Mendeley/Commons photos), the old plate is inpainted, and the white/black level, tint, blur (searched by Laplacian variance) and noise of the replaced region are matched. 4,421 composites were built (RodoSol train block 2,500, CCPD train half 1,400+, 500 photos); 1,500 go into the detector `full` set, 4,492 OCR crops into the OCR set.
* All of it is reported as **synthetic** and never mixed with real numbers.

## 4. Models and decoding

* **OCR**: `cct_s_v2_global.keras` (fast-plate-ocr CCT-S-v2, 64 x 128 RGB, 10 character slots, 37-symbol alphabet) fine-tuned with the fast-plate-ocr Keras modules (model builder, loss, metrics, default augmentation) on an in-RAM packed dataset (the shared drive was I/O-starved), 10 epochs, batch 128, AdamW lr 5e-4 cosine, bf16, no EMA (EMA cost 1.2 s/step of Python overhead on the loaded box). A 5-class **format head** (uz_private, uz_legal, uz_ev, uz_moto, foreign) is trained from the synthetic/real labels (new head, the rest initialised from the pretrained model). ONNX export uses a static batch of 1 (the torch-backend exporter fails on dynamic kernels); the reader loops over crops.
* **Format-constrained decoding** (`uz_format.constrained_decode`): the head predicts every slot independently, so the best valid reading under a layout is the per-slot arg-max restricted to digit/letter slots (exact, no beam needed) and region 01-99; the best of the three layouts wins. `auto` mode falls back to the free reading (format `foreign`/`unknown`) when the free reading is more than 6 nats more likely than the constrained one, which keeps non-Uzbek plates readable.
* **Orientation search** (`reader.py`): the crop is read in 0/180 degrees (wide crops) or 90/270 (tall crops) and the reading with the best (constrained) log-likelihood wins. Needed because about 40 % of the real Uzbek photos are upside-down.
* **Track voting** (`voting.py`): weighted log-linear pooling of the per-slot distributions of all reads of a track (weight = conf^2 x min(sqrt(box area), 200)), then constrained decoding. Baselines: best-confidence frame and string majority.
* **Detector**: RF-DETR Small, COCO-pretrained, 512 px input, 10 epochs, batch 8 x 2 accumulation, lr 1e-4; exported to ONNX (`dets` cx,cy,w,h and `labels` logits; plate = logit column 0).
* **Predictor** (`predict.py`): detector -> crop with 4 % padding -> reader (rotation search, auto decoding) -> greedy IoU plate tracks inside the predictor -> voting per track -> `Det(cls="plate", confidence=detector score, text, track_id, extra={format, format_valid, ocr_conf, rot, n_votes, head_format, ...})`. EV is flagged from the format head or a green-region colour cue. Call `reset()` between videos or cameras.

## 5. Real-UZ OCR test set: how the labels were made (declared)

* 301 crops = the GT boxes (4 % padding) of the 301 untouched `valid` originals of Roboflow Uzbek_Plates. **No text labels exist in that dataset**; they were created here.
* Pre-labelling by two independent readers: the pretrained fast-plate-ocr cct-s-v2-global (with a flag-colour orientation cue; deliberately not our fine-tuned model, to avoid anchoring on the model under test) and Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct (transformers, bf16, GPU1) on the upright crops.
* **Every crop was then read by Claude (an AI) on contact sheets** (contact sheets of 30, then blind upright sheets of 20 crops at 780 px width without proposals) and the text and orientation were recorded in `lanes/ident/labels/uz_real_test_corrections.json`. Disagreements with the VLM were re-inspected at larger scale (CLAHE zoom). **The labels are pending human spot-check**; `docs/lanes/ident_uz_review.html` shows every crop upright with its label (at least 20 % = 60 crops should be checked by a human).
* Correction rates (proposal -> final label): the pretrained reader's proposal was changed for 206 of 301 crops (68 %); the VLM proposal for 44 of 299 readable crops (14.7 %, mostly O/0 and the slotted zero read as G); the orientation proposal was changed for 92 of 301 crops (30.6 %). Consistency check: the 12 cars that appear in more than one test photo (front and rear) carry the same label in all their crops.
* Two crops were excluded as unreadable/partial (the detector box cut the plate: #138 region digits missing, #255 only a strip). Result: 299 readable labels; 293 private-layout, 6 legal-entity-layout; no EV and no motorcycle plate occurs in the real set.
* Independent confirmation: the (never real-UZ-trained) fine-tuned reader agrees with the labels on 297 of 299 crops; the two differences were re-inspected and the labels kept (one is a heavily blurred second photo of a car whose other photo is clear, one is a glare-affected region digit).

## 6. Leakage audit fix (Lane G, jury manifest)

Lane G's audit flagged 3 composite backgrounds (photos_004187 = jury_008, photos_004431 = jury_064, photos_004330 = jury_065): the Wikimedia Commons photos used as compositing backgrounds overlap the jury set. Fix (`lanes/ident/data/jury_filter.py`): every background photo is checked against `eval/manifests/jury.jsonl` by Commons title and `eval.manifest.filter_train(..., components=["jury"])` (pHash <= 8): **13 of 1,211 background photos dropped, 8 composites and 8 OCR composite crops removed** (4,500 -> 4,492), the other detector training images (4,204 Roboflow/CCPD/RodoSol images) had 0 hits. The detector `full` and the OCR `main` models that had seen them were **discarded and retrained** from the cleaned data; `python -m eval.manifest audit --components jury` on `runs/ident/det_full_small/train_files.txt` and `det_uz_only_small/train_files.txt` returns PASS. `runs/ident/ocr_main/train_files.txt` lists real photo paths for composite crops.

Reading the tables:
* **Real vs synthetic**: the real-UZ rows are real photos (299 verified crops, 287 cars, one site); the synthetic rows are rendered plates and are much harder (aggressive degradations, many crops under 80 px wide: exact match is 77 % for crops >= 200 px and about 16 % below 80 px, see `ident_results.json` ids `...synth_breakdown...`). They must not be averaged with the real rows.
* **Synthetic data on/off** (OCR): without synthetic Uzbek plates the real-UZ exact match is 86.6 % [82.3, 90.0] (96.0 % with forced constrained decoding); with them 99.0 % [97.1, 99.7]. The pretrained fast-plate-ocr models reach 33.4 % / 26.8 %.
* **Constrained decoding on/off**: no gain on the final model for real UZ (already 99.0 %), +0.2 points on synthetic; forced constraint destroys non-Uzbek plates (KZ 0 %, KG 32 %), the `auto` fall-back keeps them (KZ 99.3 %, KG 98.5 %). It helps weak models a lot (real-only: 86.6 -> 96.0 %).
* **Orientation search** is the big practical lever on this data (roughly 40 % of photos are upside-down): 47.8 % -> 98.7 %.
* **Detector**: the shipped RF-DETR Small (full variant) reaches recall 100 % on real UZ, RodoSol and CCPD-Weather and 99.2 % on CCPD-DB (dark/bright illumination) at IoU 0.5; fast-alpr gets 46.2 % on the real UZ photos (rotated plates, hood-open scenes) and 89.8 % on CCPD-DB. The Uzbek-only detector shows what the extra real foreign data + composites buy on dark scenes (65.6 -> 99.2 % on CCPD-DB). RodoSol night/rain are inside the RodoSol number (not split: the mirror has no per-image tags); RodoSol motorcycles are not in the Kaggle mirror, so two-row plates are measured **only on synthetic data** (format uz_moto: see `synth_breakdown`; weak).
* **End to end** on real UZ photos: 98.7 % [96.6, 99.5] vs 15.7 % for fast-alpr as shipped.
* **Format output**: `format_valid` = text matches a valid Uzbek layout; on the real set all 299 are valid private/legal layouts (text-layout format accuracy 100 %). The 5-class visual head is 98.9 % on synthetic, 100 % on KZ/KG/RU (foreign) but only **77.9 % on the real UZ crops** (it confuses private/legal/EV styles it only saw synthetically), so the shipped `extra["format"]` is derived from the decoded text (private/legal/moto) and only upgraded to `uz_ev`/`foreign` by the head when it is confident; EV/moto have no real examples.
* **Precision**: both models are shipped and evaluated in FP32 ONNX (training in bf16 mixed precision for OCR and AMP for RF-DETR); no FP16/BF16 export was made, so no parity check was needed.

## 8. Failure cases

`docs/lanes/failures/ident/` (faces blurred with `seen.blur`, plate pixelated on purpose; labels in `failures.json`): the 4 end-to-end misreads of 299 are single-character confusions (D/J, U/V, region digit) on blurred or glare-affected plates; there were no missed detections on the real set. Other failure modes measured: crops below ~70 px width (EM 76 % at 70 px, 33 % at 50 px), strong blur (sigma 3: 55 %), tiny synthetic moto plates, and mirrored text (not handled). Baseline failure: fast-alpr misses 54 % of the real UZ photos.

## 9. Known limits

* Real Uzbek evidence is one site (vehicle-inspection photos: close, near-frontal, large plates, phone images). **No CCTV, no IR night, no multi-plate Uzbek scenes in our real data**; the detector was trained on that same site, so its 100 % is in-domain. The stress test says OCR needs about >= 100 px plate width for > 90 %.
* Real-UZ labels are verified by Claude (AI), **pending human spot-check** (page `ident_uz_review.html`); 2 of 301 crops excluded (partial).
* EV (green region) and motorcycle layouts are synthetic only, the motorcycle layout is assumed; the EV `M DDDDDD` conflict in the research notes is not covered; trailers, diplomatic, yellow plates not generated.
* Fonts are DIN-like approximations of FE-Schrift.
* Detector input is 512 px (plates under about 20 px in 1080p frames may be missed; tiling not implemented).
* KG legal-style plates have the same text layout as Uzbek legal plates: only the visual head / auto fall-back can tell them apart.
* Voting is evaluated on synthetic tracks only (pooled 99.8 % with 10 frames vs 98.2 % best frame; 73 % vs 67 % with 3 frames). The in-predictor plate tracker is a greedy IoU matcher (reset between videos).
* Training data terms: AUTO.RIA licence not stated, RodoSol academic/non-commercial; weights are research artefacts. VIN stretch goal not attempted. The OCR ONNX has a static batch of 1.
