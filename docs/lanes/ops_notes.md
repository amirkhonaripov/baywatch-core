# Lane C: bay states and operations (ops)

Status: v1 shipped to `artifacts/ops/states/` (model_card version 0.1, `role="states"`). Every number below comes from
`runs/ops/heads_v1/*` (copied to `docs/lanes/ops_results.json`) and is regenerated by `bash lanes/ops/pipeline.sh heads_v1`.

## What it does
`lanes/ops/predict.py::get_predictor(roi=..., fixed_camera=True)` returns per-frame probabilities for
`car_present, raised, wheel_off, hood_open, person_at_car, person_under_car, work, idle, washing` from the frame and the
merged detector context (vehicle/person boxes). No video model; per-frame probabilities, Platt-calibrated; temporal
smoothing is Lane E's HMM.

Pipeline per frame:
1. Primary vehicle = the vehicle with the largest overlap with the bay ROI (or the largest vehicle without an ROI).
2. Crop = vehicle box + 25 % margin, squared -> frozen **DINOv2 ViT-L/14** (image-only pretraining; CLS + mean patch
   token, 2048-d).
3. 24 geometry cues from the detector boxes (person-vs-car overlap/gap, "head above the car's bottom edge and feet
   below it", lying aspect, ...) and from two YOLO11s part detectors trained by this lane (wheel from Ultralytics
   carparts-seg; hood open/closed from Roboflow car-hood-open).
4. One linear layer (8 outputs) on [embedding, geometry]; masked BCE over whatever labels a row has; Platt scaling per
   state on a held-out handheld validation split. Exported as a 2-node ONNX graph (`Gemm` + `Sigmoid`).
5. Rules (`lanes/ops/rules.py`, shared by predictor and evaluation): `car_present` = detector confidence of the primary
   vehicle; no vehicle -> car states capped at 0.05; `person_under_car` gated by `raised`; `idle = car_present x (1 - work)`.
6. Fixed-camera lift rule (multi-frame input): the car's bottom edge rising > 15-35 % of its height above its lowest
   position in the window while its x-extent stays put pushes `raised` up (noisy-OR).

(DINOv2-L bf16 batch 16 + ONNX head + 2 part detectors; vehicle detector excluded) -> ~USD 0.007 / 1k frames on L4.
Qwen3-VL-8B-FP8 (transformers, not vLLM): 1.8 s/frame single, 2.6 frames/s batched -> ~USD 0.34 / 1k frames on L4
(~50x). bf16 vs fp32 backbone parity on 256 test crops: embedding cosine >= 0.993, 5 / 2,048 decisions flip at 0.5.

## Failure cases (`docs/lanes/failures/ops/`, 14 images, faces blurred with `seen.blur`)
The two most confident errors per state (see `failures.json`): car_present false alarms in empty bays next to parked
cars (p 0.84-0.93); raised false alarms on bay B of two clips (p 0.88-0.96); wheel_off, hood_open, person_under_car and
work misses with p 0.02-0.23; person_at_car false alarms (p 0.96-0.97). Causes were not analysed image by image (budget);
likely contributors are multi-car frames where the ROI also contains a neighbouring car or passer-by, and the domain gap.

## Known limits
- Hand labels on CCTV and handheld checks are by one annotator (Claude, from frames); no inter-annotator agreement.
- CCTV set = 6 public timelapses at 1-5 effective fps, 458 items but only ~12 independent bay sequences: CIs are wide.
- Derived step-span labels are proxies (talking/intro outside steps, transitions excluded).
- The bay ROI is configured per camera; without an ROI the largest vehicle is used.
- Qwen3-VL ran with transformers (no vLLM: the venv install stalled on the IO-bound HDD), so its cost is an upper bound.
- K700 fetch got 5,034 of 5,593 selected clips (HF 429s); 12 CrossTask/COIN videos failed to download (403).

## For the deck
- **No real service-bay CCTV was available.** Operations were trained on YouTube/handheld video (Kinetics-700,
  CrossTask, COIN), web photos and Qwen3-VL teacher labels on public workshop timelapses.
- CCTV numbers come from **458 hand-labelled bay crops in 6 public workshop clips** (one annotator), never trained on.
- Today the fast model is good for **car present (92 %)**, **hood open (AUROC 0.93)** and **raised (AUROC 0.93)**; work,
  person-at-car and wheel-off are **not yet reliable per frame on CCTV** (62-78 % accuracy).
- A big VLM (Qwen3-VL-8B) is clearly more accurate zero-shot (AUROC 0.94-1.00) but ~50x more expensive and 1.8 s/frame;
  plan: VLM as teacher on pilot footage, small model in production. Pilot CCTV labels are the main missing ingredient.
- Under-car work is not detectable yet (AUROC 0.51, 17 examples).
