# Real-data inventory per component of the service-flow pipeline (bay-centred), research/NC/agreement-gated data allowed

Research date: 2026-10-10. Follows `stage_data.md` and `data_models.md` in the same folder (they are not repeated here; where this file says "[F-prev]" the fact was verified in those reports and not re-opened today).

What changed versus the earlier reports: licences no longer exclude a dataset, so BDD100K, DAWN, ExDark, UA-DETRAC, MIO-TCD, AI City/CityFlow, VeRi-776/VeRi-Wild, UFPR-ALPR/RodoSol-ALPR/UFPR-VeSV, CrowdHuman, COIN, Assembly101 and similar sets are now candidates. Every licence is still listed. The hackathon rule on real faces/plates of non-consenting people stays in force: the "Faces / plates" column says what each dataset contains so we can blur or show only aggregate numbers.

Strategy this file serves: prove each component separately on real public data with a measured metric, show integration on synthetic timelines plus the live demo, and say that end-to-end calibration needs pilot footage.

## Evidence tags

Access effort codes: **A** = direct download or one click (under 1 h to start). **B** = form, e-mail, click-through agreement or registration (hours to about 5 working days). **C** = needs YouTube/third-party video fetch or very large download. **D** = could not confirm it is obtainable today.

## 0. Headline findings

1. **Real data exists for every sub-cue of the flow except the bay itself.** No public dataset shows a multi-car service bay with lift/bonnet/wheel states (searched again today: Roboflow, Kaggle, Hugging Face, Zenodo, GitHub, Mendeley, arXiv/web search). The Roboflow "Carlifting" set is not a car lift set: its preview shows what looks like a scale-model car on a tabletop track (classes Tyre, Backlinear, Frontcar, Frontlinear, Midlinear, PlateNo, Stopper). [F, one preview; I on interpretation]
2. **Best real evidence for "mechanic working / car jacked / wheel off" is instructional-video datasets with step timestamps**: CrossTask (Change a Tire: 99 annotated videos, 1,014 step segments; Jack Up a Car: 89 videos, 306 segments; Add Oil to Your Car: 137 videos, 1,036 segments) and COIN (ChangeCarTire 74 videos, UseJack 44, plus 12 more car tasks). Step spans such as "jack up the car", "remove the tire", "raise the jack up", "let the jack down" can be turned into time-stamped state intervals (derived labels, so [I]). Domain gap: mostly driveway/home-garage, handheld or tripod, not CCTV. [F counts and step names]
3. **Kinetics has no car-wash class.** Kinetics-400 has only changing oil, changing wheel, checking tires, pushing car, driving car, pumping gas; Kinetics-600/700 add changing gear in car, repairing puncture, vacuuming car, steering car. Exact clip counts below. Only a handful of Kinetics-400 clips carry the YouTube CC flag (changing oil 7 of 564, checking tires 2 of 405, changing wheel 0 of 311). [F]
4. **Fixed-camera vehicle detection under night/rain has several usable real sets now that NC is allowed:** UA-DETRAC (weather/illumination labelled, but the official download page is gone and no licence statement was found), MIO-TCD localization (137,743 images, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, direct tar), NDISPark (about 250 images, day-train/night-test split, ODC-By), PKLot and CNRPark-EXT (rainy subsets). [F]
5. **Plate OCR: no real Uzbek set is large and clean, but structurally close post-Soviet sets are downloadable today without a form**: AUTO.RIA OCR crops for Kyrgyzstan (31,917 crops), Kazakhstan (9,922), Russia (57,120) and ex-USSR (38,802). The Kyrgyz label strings (example `08301AAP`) have the same `DD DDD LLL` structure as Uzbek legal-entity plates. Licence not stated, plates are real. RodoSol-ALPR (20,000 images, fixed toll cameras, day/night, clear/rain, two-row motorcycle plates, faces blurred) is the best real night/rain/two-row proof but needs a form from a university e-mail. [F]
6. **A commercial vendor (Unidata) lists 15,138 Uzbekistan plate images** inside a roughly 1.93M-image plate collection (sample downloadable on request; paid for the full set). Real plates, so evaluation only, never shown. [F on the listing]
7. **Tracking/re-ID real data is outdoor intersections only** (CityFlowV2, VeRi-776, VeRi-Wild, VehicleID, RoundaboutHD); all non-commercial; no indoor-garage re-ID set was found. Use them to prove the tracker/embedding code, not the bay. [F]
8. **Person-in-zone has good overhead options**: CEPDOF (8 overhead-fisheye classroom videos, 25,504 annotated frames), WEPDTOF (16 clips from 14 YouTube videos), HABBOF, plus CrowdHuman (non-commercial research only) and MOT17/MOT20 elevated sequences. [F]
9. **Real multi-car bay footage exists only as short public timelapses.** Six clips (30 s to 4 min, fixed overhead or CCTV-style view, 2-6+ cars) are good enough for a hand-labelled qualitative test; the best is a tyre-shop CCTV clip with a "CAM-3" overlay (YouTube `e4O3X9gli1s`, CC BY, 48 s, 3+ cars), and only that clip and one other carry a CC BY licence. None covers the whole gate-to-handover chain and none supports tracking or dwell calibration (section 6). [F-chk metadata, I content]
10. **YouTube/Kinetics access is the real bottleneck, not licences.** Kinetics-700 on Hugging Face is about 930 GB in 22 parts; CVDF hosts K400/K700-2020 tars; per-class clips need the annotation CSV plus a downloader. CrossTask and COIN ship YouTube IDs only (CrossTask has an INRIA mirror tarball for videos that vanished). [F]

## 2. Per-component details

### 2.1 Vehicle detection from fixed CCTV (indoor/garage, night, rain)

**Best picks:** NDISPark (night, clean licence), UA-DETRAC (weather labels, most CCTV-like, licence unknown), MIO-TCD (largest, CC BY-NC-SA, direct).

- **UA-DETRAC** [F paper via ar5iv]: 100 sequences, 60 train / 40 test, over 140,000 frames, 960x540 at 25 fps, Canon EOS 550D, 24 locations in the arXiv text (the author's page says 27 [S]), 8,250 vehicles, 1.21M boxes, classes car, bus, van, others; illumination/weather categories cloudy, night, sunny, rainy (per-category counts only in a figure I could not read). Paper metrics: detection AP from the PR curve at IoU 0.7 (11-point recall, PASCAL-style), tracking PR-MOTA. **Access:** `https://detrac-db.rit.albany.edu/` and `/download` both returned the University at Albany CVML lab page with no dataset links [F]; the Albany author page marks the download "under construction" [S]. Hugging Face mirror `abhineet123/ua_detrac` (README: pre-formatted for the author's own framework, tag cc-by-4.0 set by the uploader) holds `ua_detrac_training_set.zip` 5,630,247,258 bytes and `ua_detrac_test_set.zip` 4,252,838,102 bytes [F]. Other mirrors: `xujiazhen/ua_detrac`, `blues111/ua_detrac`, `ShantyCam/ua-detrac`, `lakshmenroy/UA-DETRAC` (MIT tag) [F listing]. **Licence: not found.** Real Chinese traffic, plates readable [I].
- **MIO-TCD** (Miovision/Sherbrooke) [F, tcd.miovision.com]: 786,702 images from about 8,000 traffic cameras in Canada and the US; classification set 648,959 images; localization set 137,743 images; 11 localization classes: articulated truck, bicycle, bus, car, motorcycle, motorized vehicle (too small for a specific class), non-motorized vehicle, pedestrian, pickup truck, single unit truck, work van. Licence CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Direct tar downloads (`MIO-TCD-Localization.tar`), no registration mentioned. Anonymisation not stated. Time-of-day/weather tags: not stated. HF mirrors by `ShantyCam` exist (no licence tag) [F listing].
- **NDISPark** (Zenodo 6560823) [F]: "about 250 images" (the paper is quoted as 259 in a search summary [S]), seven cameras, various weather and viewing angles, **train split is daytime, validation and test splits are night**; COCO JSON with category `car` (boxes + masks), dot annotations for counting, test counting CSV; licence ODC-By; one 118,187,828-byte zip. Paper: Ciampi et al., VISAPP 2021.
- **DAWN** (Mendeley 766ygrbt8y v3) [F]: 1,000 real traffic images in four sets (fog, snow, rain, sandstorm), vehicle boxes, CC BY-NC 3.0, "commercial use/distribution ... strictly prohibited". Per-condition counts not on the page (a figure of 200 rain images is from memory, unverified).
- **BDD100K** [F-prev licence; S counts]: dash-camera, so not a fixed-view proof. 100k videos; weather tags (clear, overcast, partly cloudy, rainy, snowy, foggy) and time-of-day tags (daytime, night, dawn/dusk) per image; a search-summary reading of the CVPR supplement gives rainy about 7,125 images but not the split [S]. HF mirrors: `dronefreak/BDD100K` (tag "other"), `dgural/bdd100k` (bsd tag), `linxxx3/bdd100k_videos`. The official doc site did not resolve from this machine.
- **ExDark** [F]: 7,363 low-light images, 12 classes, README dated 2026-09-30; academic non-commercial; a for-profit sponsor needs written confirmation; underlying photos are third-party and not licensed by ExDark.
- **AI City Challenge / CityFlow** [F]: CityFlow (CVPR 2019) has more than 3 hours of synchronised HD video, 40 cameras, 10 intersections, more than 200K boxes; CityFlowV2 (2022 Track 1): 46 cameras, 16 intersections, 3.58 h, at least 960p, mostly 10 fps, 313,931 boxes, 880 identities passing at least two cameras. The 2022 licence PDF is an NVIDIA Dataset License Agreement: after the challenge the data may be used for non-commercial academic purposes only; models trained on it may not be used for commercial or production purposes; excerpts shown in papers must not display unredacted faces or plates. 2026 (10th edition) tracks are Sim2Real multi-camera 3D, traffic-safety captioning, anomalous events, text-based person re-ID, traffic video forecasting and cross-city detection (Hafnia platform); Track 3 uses `nvidia/PhysicalAI-Traffic-Anomaly-Reasoning` (HF, cc-by-4.0, 3,670 CCTV videos from eight public sources, 44,040 chain-of-thought annotations) [F, aicitychallenge.org and HF API]. That 2026 set is CCTV video from public sources and could serve as qualitative "does the detector survive real CCTV" material; I did not inspect its content.
- **UFPR-VeSV** (arXiv 2604.05271, JBCS 2026) [F]: 24,945 images of 16,297 unique vehicles from Paraná military-police surveillance; 13 colours, 26 makes, 136 models, 14 types; plate corners and characters; nighttime (mostly infrared) = 21.5% (5,372 images per a search summary [S]); 13,842 rear-view and 11,103 frontal images; faces blurred. It is a classification/ALPR set of vehicle crops, not a detection set. Licensing agreement, academic non-commercial, send from a university e-mail; answer in 1-5 business days; repository `github.com/Lima001/UFPR-VeSV-Dataset` (no licence in GitHub metadata).
- **OD-VIRAT** (arXiv 2507.12396) [F arXiv page]: Large variant 8.7M instances in 599,996 images; Tiny 288,901 instances in 19,860 images; surveillance footage "from significant height and distance"; the arXiv page gives no licence or download link, so access is unconfirmed (D).
