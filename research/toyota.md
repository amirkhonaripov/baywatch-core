## 4. Pain points (with sources)

Honest summary: **I found very little hard, public, Uzbek-specific complaint evidence about Toyota Royal Motors.** Public reviews are overwhelmingly positive. Most pain points below are therefore structural (from market/regulation data) or inferred from job posts and campaign design.

## 5. Uzbekistan market context

**Grey imports / used market (facts, nationwide)**
- Used-car transactions: 810,000 in 2024 (-32.9%); Jan-Apr 2025 about 288,000 (~125,000 fewer YoY). Used prices falling since Sept 2023; credit and leasing mostly cover new cars. [F] https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/05/27/auto-market/
- Older estimate: 400-500k secondary-market deals/year, ~$2bn (CEER, undated, stale). [S]
- Imports: 2024 = 74,500 units ($1.32bn, -7.1%); 2025 = 79,822 ($1.1bn), **71.2% electric**; Japan supplied only 122 cars in Jan-May 2025. [S] https://frank.uz/news/import-avtomobilej-v-uzbekistan-v-2025-godu-vyros-do-79-822-na-11-mlrd/ , https://kun.uz/ru/news/2025/07/02/v-uzbekistan-za-pyat-mesyatsev-2025-goda-importirovano-boleye-18-tysyach-avtomobiley
- **Imported-car testing bottleneck at Piskent** (since 1 Nov 2024): lab capacity 100-120 cars/day, electronic queue fills in seconds, certification fees rose from ~2.6 to ~9.1 mln UZS (reported), Q1-2025 imports down ~3x, corruption suspicions. [F] https://kun.uz/en/news/2025/03/27/artificial-demand-or-system-failure-the-truth-behind-uzbekistans-car-testing-nightmare . [I]: this squeezes **parallel (grey) Toyota imports** and helps the official dealer; I found **no direct source quantifying grey Toyota imports**.
- Customs/regime [S, commercial calculators, verify]: used cars older than 3 years face prohibitive duties (40% + $3/cc) and a utilisation fee of 210 BRV (~$6,900); EVs duty-free; Jan 2026 duty rise for small engines (<=1200 cc); from 2026-27 restrictions/eco-fees on very old vehicles (sources disagree). Sources: https://www.autostrada.uz/rastamozhka-uzbekistan/ ; https://kun.uz/ru/news/2026/01/06/dlya-malolitrajnyx-avtomobiley-povysilis-tamojyennyye-poshliny
- Registration fee for a petrol car jumped 68x to 5.4 mln UZS from April 2024, so many owners drive on **power of attorney without re-registering** - a data-integrity problem for used-car valuation. [F] gazeta.uz link above.
- State trade-in scheme for cars older than 15 years (decree reported Nov 2025): https://uz.kursiv.media/en/2025-11-25/uzbekistan-introduces-trade-in-program-for-cars-over-15-years-old/ [S]

**Insurance (OSAGO / KASKO)**
- OSAGO reformed 1 Jan 2026: max cover doubled 40 -> 80 mln UZS (property damage up to 28 mln, health up to 52 mln); policy price in Tashkent ~192k UZS; regulator said 2,700 complaints (>50% of all) were about **payout delays and too-low limits**; insurers paid 2.8 bn UZS after complaints; a unified insurance billing system from 1 Apr 2026. [S] https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/02/06/car-insurance/ ; https://www.uzdaily.uz/ru/v-uzbekistane-strakhovye-kompanii-vyplatili-potrebiteliam-28-mlrd-sumov-posle-zhalob/

[S] https://www.autostrada.uz/kasko-reshaet/ . Which insurers do automated photo assessment: **not found.**
- Dealers handle accident repairs for KASKO clients: a natural insurer-dealer data flow, **but no source describing it for Toyota UZ**.

**Road safety / cameras / plates**

[S] https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/10/27/fines/ ; https://daryo.uz/ru/2026/04/23/v-uzbekistane-voditeli-poluchili-shtrafnye-bally/

## 7. CV opportunities (ranked)

Scoring against the judging criteria: technical complexity 25, innovation / hard to copy 25, impact & practicality 25 (real problem, who pays, evidence, 3-month pilot), presentation 15, business/ROI 10.

**Important caveat on evidence:** for most opportunities below, the *public* evidence of the pain at Toyota Royal Motors is thin. The strongest evidence is (a) the new Qushbegi express-service layout, (b) 10-point inspection campaigns, (c) the lack of a trade-in/CPO programme, (d) the Toyota Academy "one standard" goal. Be honest about this in the pitch and propose to measure the pain in the pilot.

### #1. "Vehicle Condition Passport": phone walk-around at service intake, handover and trade-in (one pipeline, three processes)
- **Problem:** Disputes "who scratched it" at service intake / after detailing or Ziebart; no objective condition record at handover or trade-in; manual photo-taking is inconsistent across dealers.
- **Evidence:** Qushbegi express-service zone is a *visible* service flow (customers watch) [F: home page]; Toyota Academy aims at one service standard [F]; Ziebart/detailing rolling out to all dealers (more paint/body touchpoints) [F: Telegram]; Toyota's own US dealers adopt UVeye/Ravin for exactly this and say time-stamped scans "help dispute damage claims" [S: Motor1]. No Uzbek complaints found. [Honest gap]
- **Owner unit:** Toyota Royal Motors **service reception (приёмщик / service consultant)**, with the distributor's after-sales/warranty department as a sponsor (standard for all dealers).
- **Process changed:** vehicle reception (check-in) and handover; produces a signed digital condition report sent by Telegram.
- **Business value (rough, [I], not measured):** fewer goodwill repairs / disputes; shorter reception time; upsell evidence (found scratches -> detailing/Ziebart). Pilot KPI: minutes per reception, number of disputed claims, attach rate of body/paint services.
- **Data:** public: CarDD (4,000 images, ~9,000 instances, 6 classes: dent, scratch, crack, glass shatter, lamp broken, tyre flat; non-commercial licence; https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.00945), Roboflow Universe car-damage sets. Own: we can shoot 200-500 walk-arounds of Camry / RAV4 / Prado / LC300 (and the team's own cars) within days; Toyota bodies are glossy white/black, hard cases (reflections) to be tested.
- **Hackathon feasibility:** High for detection + before/after change detection on two videos of the same car (the "diff" is the novel part: pose-aligned, flags only *new* damage); plate OCR links to the customer record. Medium for severity/cost. **Hard-to-copy angle:** per-dealer before/after matching, UZ plate format, Telegram-first UX, offline-capable.
- **Privacy note:** announce scanning to customers (US backlash precedent).

### #2. Service-lane ANPR + dwell-time / bay analytics from existing CCTV
- **Problem:** Reception is manual; no data on how long cars wait; new multi-site operation (2 Toyota sites + BYD sites) needs comparable KPIs; "pushing visits" campaigns need measurement.
- **Evidence:** Qushbegi with express zone and diagnostic/technical zones [F]; service consultant role "increase service sales volume" [F, BYD sister dealer]; global analogue SKAIVISION at a Toyota dealer [S]. Direct evidence of queues at Toyota Royal Motors: **not found**.
- **Owner unit:** service manager / general manager of Toyota Royal Motors (service department).
- **Process changed:** arrival -> check-in (plate recognised, customer record + next-service due + voucher auto-shown on advisor's screen) -> bay assignment -> ready-for-pickup.
- **Value:** minutes saved per visit; fewer lost walk-ins; voucher redemption tracking (the 300,000 UZS vouchers are a measurable campaign) [F].
- **Data:** UZ plate format 01 A 123 BC / 01 123 ABC (see section 5); public ANPR sets exist (Roboflow, CCPD is Chinese, UFPR-ALPR Brazilian); better to collect 300-500 own plate crops in Tashkent parking within a day.
- **Feasibility:** High (YOLO detector + OCR + tracker). Moderate novelty alone, strong when combined with #1 (plate triggers the passport).

### #3. Visual "10-point check" assistant (tyres, brake pads/discs, wipers, lights, leaks) from technician phone photos
- **Problem:** The 10-point free diagnostic is the core of two 2026 campaigns [F]; quality and upsell depend on technician skill; skills are scarce (Academy, job posts [F]).
- **Evidence:** campaigns [F]; Toyota Academy "one standard" [F]; BYD-sister job posts for diagnosticians [F].
- **Owner unit:** Toyota Royal Motors service workshop (master/technician) + distributor's Toyota Academy (standard).
- **Process changed:** inspection step in maintenance: photo -> automatic condition rating (green/amber/red) -> customer-friendly report in Telegram -> recommended work order.
- **Value:** higher conversion of checks to paid work; consistent standard across dealers; training aid.
- **Data:** public tyre-tread / tyre-sidewall / worn-tyre datasets (Kaggle, Roboflow) - quality varies; own data: photograph tyres on any cars within days.
- **Feasibility:** Medium-High for tyre tread depth (with a coin/reference object or phone LiDAR), medium for brakes. Strong demo, moderate novelty.

### #4. Photo-based trade-in / used-car appraisal for a Toyota Certified Used programme
- **Problem:** No official trade-in or CPO programme found for Toyota in Uzbekistan, while neighbours (KZ, KG) run "Toyota Tested". Used market shrank 33% in 2024 but is huge (810k deals) and new-car credit is the main financing [F]; registration-by-power-of-attorney blurs vehicle history [F].
- **Evidence:** absence of programme [S/absence-of-evidence]; used-market numbers [F gazeta.uz]; Kazakhstan template [S].
- **Owner unit:** dealer's sales department (trade-in desk) + distributor's sales & marketing (programme owner).
- **Process changed:** trade-in valuation: from "manager looks and guesses" to photo-guided appraisal (damage map + odometer OCR + VIN/plate OCR) giving a price band and a repair-cost estimate.
- **Value:** converts used-car owners into new Toyota buyers (Camry / RAV4 hybrids); reduces valuation disputes; creates stock for a CPO programme. Size cannot be quantified from public data.
- **Data:** CarDD etc. for damage; used-car prices from avtoelon.uz / olx.uz / auto.uz listings (can be scraped by the team, check terms); no UZ-specific labelled valuation set exists [I].
- **Feasibility:** Medium: damage detection easy, price model needs a small scraped dataset. Highest "who pays" clarity if the distributor wants a CPO programme, but depends on a decision that has not been publicly made.

### #5. Parts counter visual lookup (photo of old part -> OEM part number / genuine vs. non-genuine check)
- **Problem:** Parts specialist role stresses stock control and speed; counterfeit parts exist nationwide with weak seller liability [S]; distributor is planning a centralised warehouse [F: Mar 2025 plan].
- **Evidence:** job post for parts specialist (BYD sister) [F]; counterfeit climate [S]. No Toyota UZ parts wait data found.
- **Owner unit:** dealer parts department / distributor Central Parts Depot (listed in history captions, no details) [F].
- **Process changed:** walk-in parts identification and VIN/label checks.
- **Data:** hard. Genuine Toyota parts catalogue images are not freely licensed; you would have to shoot your own (e.g. 30-50 common service items: filters, pads, plugs).
- **Feasibility:** Low-Medium for a 48-hour build; image retrieval over a small set is doable. Weak innovation story unless paired with label/packaging authenticity (UV/hologram), which is speculative.

### #6. Warranty claim triage from photos (customer sends photo of defect, dealer/distributor decides warranty vs. paid)
- **Problem/evidence:** Warranty terms (3 yr/100k, hybrid battery 8 yr) [F]; no public warranty disputes found. Speculative.
- **Owner:** distributor warranty department. **Feasibility:** Low-Medium; no data. Include only as an extension of #1.

### #7. Showroom / stock-yard analytics (footfall, test-drive conversion, yard stock count)
- **Evidence:** Telegram shows ~5 cars "in stock" scale (tilda site) [F]: stock is small, so yard counting is low value. **Feasibility:** easy, **value:** low, **novelty:** low. Not recommended.
