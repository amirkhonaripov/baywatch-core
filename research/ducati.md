### 4.2 Regulation and enforcement (relevant to riders)
- Mopeds and scooters must be **registered and have plates since 1 Oct 2025**; riding requires category A or A1. A1 introduced end of March 2026 (up to 25 cm3 or 0.25 kW and 25 km/h). Licence A: age 18, valid 10 years, Tashkent total cost about 3-5M UZS. Driving-school training optional from 1 Feb 2026. Practical exam is **video recorded**. [V] (Kun.uz, Spot.uz)
- **Raids on couriers** (Oct 2026): 23,785 violations by motorcycle/scooter/moped riders since the start of 2026 (Yandex Uzbekistan figure), about 4.7bn UZS in fines; only about 8% were missing documents; most were reflectors, headlights, tinting, passengers. SBDD says 4,312 moped/scooter violations in nine months, about 20x the ~300 in all of 2025. [V]
- National helmet law: yes, drivers and passengers, all road types; **no helmet standard specified**; helmet wearing rate **not reported to WHO**. [V] WHO 2023 country profile.
- Helmet fine: prava.uz table shows 165,000 UZS (0.5 BRV); another figure implies 1 BRV is about 412,000 UZS, so 0.5 BRV would be about 206,000; table may be outdated. [I]
- Speeding on a motorcycle: Art. 128-3, 9-15 BRV (3.37M-6.18M UZS) for the 310 km/h and 210 km/h cases; detection came from rider-posted videos. [V]
- Night racing and noise: Tashkent GUVD asked riders not to race or ride in groups at night (2018 article, only a request, no sanction). No ban of motorcycles in city zones found. [V/NF]
- Motorcycle plates: motorcycles "000 HH", mopeds "000 HHH" (square, single rear plate); a March 2026 AvtoRaqam article shows "123 MM 01" for motorcycles/scooters, a discrepancy I could not resolve. [V with conflict]
- Insurance: compulsory MTPL (OSGO) revised 1 Jan 2026, limit doubled from 40M to 80M UZS; motorcycle tariff 72,000 UZS in Tashkent/region (60,000 elsewhere) per Daryo. Voluntary KASKO for motorcycles in Uzbekistan: **[NF]**. [V/NF]
- Import duties, utilisation fee for motorcycles in 2025-26: **[NF]** (only 2020 figures and EV changes found).
- Theft of motorcycles in Tashkent, grey imports of Ducati: **[NF]**. One older advertorial (Motosalon.uz, 2020) shows parallel import of North American bikes with checks for "hidden defects (accidents, floods, stunts)". [V]

## 6. CV opportunities (ranked)

Scoring logic: fit with the judging criteria (technical depth, hard to copy, real owner and process, 3-month pilot, data availability) and honesty about evidence.

### Rank 1: "Ducati Condition Passport" (photo-based bike inspection at handover, service intake, test-fleet return and trade-in)
- **Problem**: Each bike is a $18k-$50k+ asset. Condition is disputed at four moments: arrival from Italy (transit damage vs. Ducati claim), test-ride return (the test fleet is then sold as "ex-test-fleet with dealer warranty"), service intake (what damage existed before?), and used/trade-in appraisal. No photo evidence trail is visible anywhere in the channel.
- **Evidence**: ex-test-fleet bikes sold at 534-1,147 km with dealer warranty (https://t.me/s/DucatiUzbekistan, #437/#439/#441); 2-year warranty and official invoicing (https://www.centrum-air.com/en/blogs/ducati-uzbekistan); service accepts "other brands" (#450); Ducati Approved uses a 35-point inspection (https://powersportsbusiness.com/top-stories/2018/10/03/uk-ducati-launches-certified-pre-owned-program/); parallel imports are checked for hidden accident/flood/stunt damage (https://repost.uz/motosalon, advertorial, 2020). WHO notes Uzbekistan has no safety standards for used-vehicle imports (https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/country-profiles/road-safety/road-safety-2023-uzb.pdf). Pain is **[I]** from structure; no customer complaint found.
- **Unit and process**: Ducati Uzbekistan aftersales and used-bike sales. Processes changed: PDI (pre-delivery inspection), test-ride return check, service check-in, warranty claim packet, ex-demo pricing, trade-in valuation.
- **How it works**: 6-8 guided photos or a 30-second walk-around video per bike, matched to VIN, with change detection against the baseline (scratches, dents, cracked fairing, bent levers/pegs, tank dents, scuffed carbon/paint), and a report with severity and repair cost class. Used-bike mode adds crash signatures (scraped bar-ends, frame sliders, replaced panels, fresh paint overspray).
- **Business value (rough)**: avoids disputed damage cost on test fleet and transit; faster, more trusted pricing of ex-demo and trade-ins; evidence for warranty. No loss figures available **[NF]**. Illustration only: one avoided dispute over a damaged fairing set on a Panigale V4 S (price 517M UZS) is plausibly a four-to-five-figure USD item **[I, assumption]**.
- **Data**: no public motorcycle-damage dataset of note found. Could collect within days: the showroom has bikes on display, a service area, and test-fleet bikes; add staged damage with removable scuff/dent stickers and photos of owners' bikes at intake. Public car-damage datasets can pretrain the damage head (e.g. CarDD, not verified here). Used-bike photos from OLX Tashkent are public (check terms).
- **Feasibility (hackathon)**: High for before/after change detection with a fixed capture protocol; medium for open-world crash-history inference (state it as "risk score", not "verdict"). Hard-to-copy: motorcycle-specific parts taxonomy (Ducati fairings, Brembo/Öhlins components, exposed trellis frame), VIN-linked baselines, and local workflow.

### Rank 2: Pre-season "spring check-in" and tyre/consumable triage from owner photos
- **Problem**: Short riding season concentrates service and tyre demand in spring; owners need to know what their bike needs before they arrive; service capacity is limited (Mon-Fri only, one two-week shutdown in 2025).
- **Evidence**: pre-season package (diagnostics, consumables, tyres, balancing) (#444); the "only balancing machine in Uzbekistan" and Pirelli/Michelin stock (#412, #450); season opening is mid-April (https://www.afisha.uz/uz/gorod/2026/04/15/moto-sezon); service hours (centrum-air). Winter length is general knowledge, not sourced **[I]**.
- **Unit and process**: Ducati service department: appointment planning and quoting before intake.
- **How it works**: owner uploads photos of tyres (tread, DOT date code OCR, cracks), chain slack and wear, brake discs and pads, leaks, corrosion, battery terminals; the model estimates the service package, parts, and time slot; the system pre-books capacity.
- **Value**: higher tyre/consumables attach rate, smoother workshop loading, fewer no-shows. No numbers available **[NF]**.
- **Data**: collect in service bay within days; DOT code OCR and tyre sidewall text are easy; tread depth from photos is harder (use reference coin/gauge in frame).
- **Feasibility**: medium-high. Technical depth moderate (OCR, wear regression, multi-photo fusion).

### Rank 3: Test-ride gate (licence + protective gear check before a demo bike leaves)
- **Problem**: Demo rules already require category A, gear and 2+ years' experience; checking is manual. A crash on a 400-500M UZS demo bike is costly, and licensing rules are changing (A/A1 split, new training rules).
- **Evidence**: test-ride requirements (#323); licence categories and video-recorded exams (https://kun.uz/ru/news/2025/11/03/kak-poluchit-prava-na-skutery-i-mototsikly-i-skolko-eto-stoit); fast-bike incidents (https://kun.uz/en/news/2025/01/04/motorcyclist-in-tashkent-fined-for-driving-at-310-kmh).
- **Unit and process**: Ducati sales / test-ride desk. Process: pre-ride compliance and liability record.
- **How it works**: phone/tablet at the desk: OCR licence category and validity, face match to the licence photo, detect helmet (fastened), jacket, gloves, boots, back protector, closed-toe footwear; writes a time-stamped record to the CRM; also logs bike condition photo out/in (links to Rank 1).
- **Value**: reduce demo crashes and disputes; documentation for insurer (insurance cover for demo fleet: **[NF]**). Handling of personal data and consent needs care.
- **Data**: helmet/PPE datasets are public (e.g. AI City Challenge 2023 Track 5, helmet detection; 100 train + 100 test videos, 1920x1080, 7 classes: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023W/AICity/html/Naphade_The_7th_AI_City_Challenge_CVPRW_2023_paper.html); Uzbek licence layout we can photograph ourselves (team's own cards, with consent).
- **Feasibility**: high, but moderate technical novelty. Best as a module inside Rank 1.

### Rank 4: Marketplace brand and used-bike monitor (fake "Ducati" listings, grey-import and genuine used Ducati valuation)
- **Problem**: OLX shows Ducati-named listings at 16.7M-18.5M UZS (clearly not genuine big Ducatis) next to genuine ones at 226M-340M UZS; buyers can confuse the brand.
- **Evidence**: OLX search results (https://www.olx.uz/transport/moto/tashkent/q-s320/, search snippets for "Ducati 400cc" and "moto ducatti"), the official-importer OLX profile with one listing (https://www.olx.uz/list/user/snLvC/). The counterfeit or lookalike conclusion is **[I]** (I did not open the listings).
- **Unit and process**: Ducati Uzbekistan marketing and pre-owned sales: monitoring, brand protection, price benchmarking for trade-ins.
- **How it works**: daily crawl of listings (check marketplace terms), classify Ducati model/year from photos (genuine vs. lookalike), read plate/VIN/odometer from images, flag suspicious ads, output market price bands for trade-ins.
- **Value**: brand protection plus better trade-in pricing. Hard to quantify **[NF]**.
- **Data**: public listing images (terms and privacy to be checked); Ducati press images for genuine references.
- **Feasibility**: medium. Fine-grained model recognition is a real technical problem; scraping and terms of service are a risk.

### Rank 5: Road-hazard mapping from rider phone or action-cam video for the Ducati community
- **Problem**: Potholes and ruined asphalt are a recurring issue for all vehicles in Tashkent; premium bikes have low ground clearance and costly tyres; the group rides (We Ride As One route to Amirsoy) would benefit from vetted routes.
- **Evidence**: hokim ordering pothole repair within 10 days and asking residents to submit photos (https://anhor.uz/news/roads/); complaints of punctured tyres (https://anhor.uz/vzglyad-iznutri/tashkent-86/); WHO and ATO data gaps on motorcyclist safety (above). No motorcycle-specific road complaints found **[NF]**.
- **Unit and process**: Ducati community/marketing (and, as a customer-facing add-on, the city road authority). Process: route planning for group rides; data donated to hokimiyat.
- **How it works**: phone mounted on bike -> pothole, crack, gravel, manhole, and sand detection -> geotagged map -> ride route scoring.
- **Value**: community engagement, safety, PR. Who pays is weak (Ducati marketing budget or city partner) **[I]**.
- **Data**: RDD2022 (47,420 images; Japan, India, Czech, Norway, US, China; CC BY-SA 4.0) https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/RDD2022_-_The_multi-national_Road_Damage_Dataset_released_through_CRDDC_2022/21431547; plus our own Tashkent video in a few days. Smartphone-to-road domain shift to be checked.
- **Feasibility**: high technically, low defensibility (many open-source solutions exist), weaker on "real problem who pays".

## 8. Surprising or notable findings
1. The Ducati importer sells **ex-test-fleet bikes with only 500-1,150 km** and a dealer warranty. A condition record for this flow would be a clean story.
2. **WHO's 2023 profile for Uzbekistan has blanks** for road-user death split, powered two/three-wheeler registrations and helmet-wearing rate. There is no authoritative number for motorcycle deaths in Tashkent; we could not find one either.
3. **Prices on Ducati's own Telegram fell 10-17% during 2025**. The cause is not stated.
4. Two official 2025 fatality figures differ (2,188 vs. 1,854); both are published in the same week.
5. October 2026 news is dominated by **moped/scooter raids on couriers** (23,785 violations since January), not by premium motorcycles; Ducati is not affected directly but regulatory attention to two-wheelers is rising.
6. Fast-bike incidents (310 km/h, 210 km/h) were caught from rider-posted video, not from cameras; this is a reputational risk for the superbike segment.
7. The Ducati showroom shares **Zargar 4 / "Tashkent City"** with Toyota Royal Motors; no legal link was found online.
8. Ducati itself has **no public camera-vision programme**. Its safety work is radar (Bosch), IMU-based algorithms (DVO), airbag vests and V2X; AI is used in MotoGP data and a new engine-stress predictive maintenance model for the Desmo450 MX (Apr 2026). A vision tool for the dealer process does not duplicate any announced Ducati product.
