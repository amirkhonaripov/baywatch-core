# BYD Royal Motors (Uzbekistan): partner research for the CV hackathon

Research date: 2026-10-10. Method: web search plus page fetches (English, Russian). Telegram, Instagram comments, 2GIS and avtoelon.uz were not readable by the tools, so customer-complaint evidence is thinner than we wanted.

Labels used throughout:
- **[FACT]** stated in a source we fetched or saw in a search result (URL given).
- **[CLAIM]** stated by the company or an advertorial about itself (self-reported, promotional).
- **[INFERENCE]** our reasoning; not confirmed by a source.
- **[NOT FOUND]** searched and did not find it.

## 4. Pain points (with sources)

### 4.1 Evidence about BYD Royal Motors itself
- **[FACT]** Public ratings are very strong (5.0), with only a minority negative on waiting time. Source: https://maps.yango.com/org/48484924346/ . **No direct complaint about BYD Chigatoy was found.** (Telegram/2GIS/Instagram comments not accessible via our tools.)
- **[CLAIM]** Service-booking form asks "bought from official dealer?" ( https://byd-rm.uz/service ), so the process distinguishes official vs non-official customers. **[INFERENCE]** This signals grey-import cars arrive at service.

### 4.2 Sector and BYD-in-Uzbekistan pain points
1. **Grey imports of BYD, no warranty for them.** **[FACT]** BYD asked the government to curb "unregulated" imports; official dealers will not give warranty service to grey cars; BYD said some grey cars were modified and origin cannot be verified. A reporter estimated about two-thirds of Song DM-i seen in Tashkent were unofficial imports, spotting them by Chinese-character emblems. Sources: https://vaib.uz/2024/09/19/byd-ne-budet-okazyvat-garantijnoe-obsluzhivanie-serym-avtomobilyam-zavezennym-v-uzbekistan/ , https://podrobno.uz/cat/obchestvo/v-kompanii-byd-rasskazali-pro-strategiyu-razvitiya-biznesa/ , https://xn----7sbbeeptbfadjdvm5ab9bqj.xn--p1ai/2024/05/19/byd-v-uzbekistane-2/ . Import rules since 2024 tightened; recycling fee on imported EVs raised from 1 May 2025, locally built EVs exempt until 2030 (https://birbozor.uz/blog/elektromobil-ozbekistonda-2026, via search summary).
2. **Parts waiting and long time in service.** **[FACT]** March 2026: a buyer's BYD Seal 6 (bought at BYD Megawatt, a different dealer) failed 3 times in 10 days; parts were ordered from China; the dealer kept the car and refused replacement (Kun.uz headline, body not retrievable: https://kun.uz/ru/news/2026/03/12/3-raza-vyshla-iz-stroya-za-10-dney-v-tashkente-kuplennyy-byd-seal-6-okazalsya-brakovannym ; details from a search summary, not verified). A user review on magg.uz says maintenance is good but parts are not always in stock (https://magg.uz/ru/tashkent/byd-service, search summary).
3. **Charging reliability and legality.** **[FACT]** Dec 2024 mass disconnection of chargers in Tashkent; Mar 2026: more than 300 of 1,300+ fast chargers inspected, 101 temporarily disconnected for missing documents, 62 with no grid contract; operators claim 2,000+ stations nationwide (about 1,000 Tokbor) serving about 150,000 EV/hybrid vehicles; a deputy hokim called it "a losing story for everyone". Sources: https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/03/13/ev-charging-station/ , https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/03/02/chargers-tashkent/
4. **Chargers occupied after charging ends.** **[FACT]** April 2025: drivers plug in and leave; operators set idle fees (average 500 UZS per minute); consumer-protection committee says such fees are illegal without permits; petrol cars also block bays. Source: https://vaib.uz/2025/04/03/parkovka-ili-podzaryadka-v-tashkente-razgoraetsya-konflikt-mezhdu-vladelczami-elektrozapravok-i-voditelyami-elektromobilej/
5. **EV insurance and total-loss uncertainty.** **[FACT]** Uzbek insurers have EV products (EUROASIA covers Tesla, BYD, Zeekr incl. battery in insured events; Uzbekinvest "Green KASKO", Feb 2025, battery terms not stated). Tariffs for EUROASIA KASKO LITE are 1.3% of value up to 500 million UZS, from an Avtostrada article about a year old. Russian sources say a traction battery can exceed 60% of car value, so crashed EVs are often written off. No Uzbekistan-specific total-loss or battery-repair-cost data found. Sources: https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/02/05/uzbekinvest/ , https://www.autostrada.uz/skolko-stoit-kasko/ , https://tarantas.news/posts/id70874-odna-batareja-mozhet-stoit-bolshe-60-mashiny-pochemu-elektrokar-posle-dtp-legche-spisat
6. **Used-EV valuation.** **[FACT]** IMRI data show used EV prices falling in 2024 to mid-2025 (e.g. used BYD Han average about 34,100 to 32,500 USD June 2025; Song Plus EV Champion about 25,500 USD). Guides stress battery SoH over mileage and note dashboard SoH can be manipulated after repairs. Sources: https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/07/11/auto/ , https://tengrinews.kz/tengriautouseful/proverit-batareyu-elektromobilya-gibrida-pokupkoy-polnyiy-603172/ . Tashkent already has OLX services advertising battery diagnostics for BYD/Zeekr/Tesla.
7. **Competition and price pressure.** **[FACT]** Chery hybrids, Deepal, Geely, Lixiang, Voyah now compete below 400 million UZS; China-side BYD sales were falling; local BYD share rose to 9.4% though. Source: ABA release in 1.5.
8. **Winter/summer range, battery degradation.** **[NOT FOUND]** Uzbekistan-specific owner evidence. General: cold cuts LFP range; BYD mentions AC compressor upgrade for local heat (https://podrobno.uz/cat/obchestvo/v-kompanii-byd-rasskazali-pro-strategiyu-razvitiya-biznesa/).
9. **Delivery damage at handover.** **[NOT FOUND]** (no Uzbek source). Logistics are CBU imports from China plus Jizzakh assembly, so the risk exists by nature **[INFERENCE]**.
10. **Insurance claims after accident / body-repair cost, HV battery damage assessment.** **[NOT FOUND]** local complaint sources. A search summary found no Uzbek reports on BYD body repair delays.

## 6. CV opportunities (ranked)

Ranking logic: owner clearly inside the partner, real evidence, data we can collect in days, defensible against a generic "damage detection" demo, 3-month pilot at one site (BYD Chigatoy, Tashkent). Business values are rough **[INFERENCE]** and not sourced.

### Rank 1. BYD "digital vehicle passport": handover/PDI + service-intake photo inspection with warranty-eligibility and grey-import check
- **Problem:** Cars arrive from China or Jizzakh, are handed over, and later return for warranty. Disputes arise on "who caused the scratch", and the service desk has to judge whether a car is an official-market car (warranty valid) or a grey import (no warranty).
- **Evidence:** BYD says official dealers will not warranty grey cars and that origin cannot be verified (https://vaib.uz/2024/09/19/byd-ne-budet-okazyvat-garantijnoe-obsluzhivanie-serym-avtomobilyam-zavezennym-v-uzbekistan/ , https://podrobno.uz/cat/obchestvo/v-kompanii-byd-rasskazali-pro-strategiyu-razvitiya-biznesa/ ); a reporter spotted grey cars by Chinese emblems (https://xn----7sbbeeptbfadjdvm5ab9bqj.xn--p1ai/2024/05/19/byd-v-uzbekistane-2/ ); Royal Motors' own service form asks "bought from official dealer" (https://byd-rm.uz/service ); Namangan Head of Service and a service training centre being hired (hh.uz link above). Direct evidence of handover-damage disputes at Royal Motors: **[NOT FOUND]**.
- **Owner unit:** After-sales / service department of BYD Chigatoy (Royal Motors group), with new-car PDI team and warranty admin.
- **Process that changes:** service intake and warranty acceptance; new-car handover checklist (currently manual/paper **[INFERENCE]**).
- **Business value:** fewer warranty claims paid on non-eligible cars; time saved at intake; dispute evidence with timestamps. Cannot be quantified from sources.
- **Data:** public car damage sets exist (e.g. CarDD, Roboflow Universe car damage collections; recalled from general knowledge, not verified in this research); VIN plate/emblem/screen-language/sticker OCR data we can shoot ourselves in a few days at dealer lots or on the street (BYD cars are common in Tashkent).
- **Hackathon feasibility:** High. A phone walk-around with damage detection, VIN/plate/emblem/language-of-infotainment check and a generated PDF passport is demoable. Differentiator: BYD-specific market-origin checks (emblem script, infotainment language, label sticker format), which generic damage tools lack.

### Rank 2. Crash triage for EVs: high-voltage-battery risk and total-loss prediction from photos (insurer and dealer body shop)
- **Problem:** After an accident the key question is whether the HV battery pack is at risk and whether repair or write-off is cheaper. This is slow and opaque; the battery can be 30 to over 60% of vehicle value (foreign sources).
- **Evidence:** battery-cost range from foreign insurers (https://tarantas.news/posts/id70874-odna-batareja-mozhet-stoit-bolshe-60-mashiny-pochemu-elektrokar-posle-dtp-legche-spisat ); Uzbek EV KASKO exists with battery covered in insured events (https://www.autostrada.uz/skolko-stoit-kasko/ , https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/02/05/uzbekinvest/ ); 109k EVs in the country; dealer promises post-accident computer diagnostics at one rival service (search summary). Uzbekistan-specific complaints about total-loss disputes or body-repair delays: **[NOT FOUND]**.
- **Owner unit:** service/body-shop and insurance desk of the dealer; a partner insurer as payer.
- **Process:** claims estimate and repair-or-write-off decision; parts order (so shorter time with parts-from-China wait).
- **Value:** shorter estimate cycle; fewer unnecessary battery pack swaps; negotiation evidence. Unquantified.
- **Data:** CarDD/other damage datasets for generic damage; battery-underbody impact labels would need our own annotation (limited).
- **Feasibility:** Medium. Damage segmentation is doable in days; battery-risk scoring needs rules from repair manuals (not public in Uzbek) and is the hard, defensible part. Can be shipped as "damage zones to parts list to HV inspection flag" with honest uncertainty.

### Rank 3. Used-EV / trade-in appraisal from photos plus battery-health evidence
- **Problem:** Resale value uncertainty for used EVs; SoH cannot be trusted from the dashboard alone.
- **Evidence:** IMRI shows EV used prices falling 2024 to mid-2025 (https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2025/07/11/auto/ ); guides warn about manipulated battery indicators (https://tengrinews.kz/tengriautouseful/proverit-batareyu-elektromobilya-gibrida-pokupkoy-polnyiy-603172/ ); Tashkent market already sells independent battery checks on OLX. Trade-in is **not** mentioned by BYD Chigatoy (**[NOT FOUND]**), so it would be a new service, not a fix to an existing one.
- **Owner unit:** Used-car / trade-in desk (would need to be created) or sales + F&I; also banks and leasing companies.
- **Process:** appraisal of incoming vehicles; certified-pre-owned programme.
- **Value:** residual-value confidence supports 16% subsidised loans for new EV buyers (if buyers can trade in).
- **Data:** avtoelon.uz/OLX listings (scraping needs checking for terms); our own photo set is limited. Photo-only price estimation is noisy; combine with a dashboard photo OCR (odometer, SoH if shown, range).
- **Feasibility:** Medium. Condition grading plus odometer/dashboard OCR is easy; price model is weak without transaction data.

### Rank 4. Service-lane plate recognition linked to customer record + bay and queue analytics
- **Problem:** Intake time, waiting experience and bay utilisation; with a multi-site group (Tashkent, Namangan, Samarkand, Kokand) managers need comparable metrics.
- **Evidence:** only 73% positive on waiting time (11 reviews, https://maps.yango.com/org/48484924346/ ); online booking slots 09:00 to 17:00 (https://byd-rm.uz/service ); hiring service heads, a trainer and a dealer-marketing lead (hh.uz link). No complaint about long queues at Royal Motors found.
- **Owner unit:** After-sales manager; group dealer-marketing/CRM.
- **Process:** arrival recognition to pre-filled work order and customer greeting; bay occupancy to promise times.
- **Value:** reduced intake time and no-shows; data to justify extra bays. Unquantified.
- **Data:** we can film at any lot; Uzbek plates have fixed formats (green region field for EVs) so OCR is tractable; public ANPR datasets exist but not Uzbek-specific.
- **Feasibility:** High technically, but low novelty (commodity ANPR). Best as a module inside Rank 1.

### Rank 5. Charging bay occupancy and idle-vehicle detection (dealer-owned or partner charging)
- **Problem:** Cars sit on chargers after charge completion; idle fees are disputed as illegal; chargers are also blocked by petrol cars.
- **Evidence:** https://vaib.uz/2025/04/03/parkovka-ili-podzaryadka-v-tashkente-razgoraetsya-konflikt-mezhdu-vladelczami-elektrozapravok-i-voditelyami-elektromobilej/ ; mass disconnections in 2026 (https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/03/13/ev-charging-station/ ). BYD discussed a fast-charging network with the ministry (https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/07/09/byd/ ), but BYD Royal Motors owning chargers: **[NOT FOUND]**.
- **Owner unit:** None confirmed inside BYD Royal Motors today; would be a new unit or a charging-operator partner (Tokbor, Megawatt Energy, Spectre, Quwatt).
- **Value:** operator revenue and driver availability; neutral to BYD's retail P&L.
- **Data:** easy to film chargers; hardest part is a real pilot partner.
- **Feasibility:** High for a prototype; weakest fit with "partner owns the process" criterion. Good as an extension if we show a BYD-branded charger station at the dealership.

### Rank 6. Stock-yard and showroom inventory (vehicle counting, PDI before delivery, key/colour/VIN reconciliation)
- **Problem:** Dealer yards across 30 BYD outlets; stock visibility; parts warehouse (a warehouse keeper is being hired).
- **Evidence:** only the warehouse keeper vacancy; no complaint found. **[INFERENCE]** value.
- **Feasibility:** High, low novelty. Fold into Rank 1.

### Rank 7. Driver monitoring / dashcam analytics / road-condition mapping for fleet customers (taxi, rental)
- **Evidence:** Yandex Go Electro tariff exists (https://www.spot.uz/ru/2024/05/21/yandex-go-ev/ ); BYD pushes Linghui taxi brand in China only. Local fleet deal for BYD Royal Motors: **[NOT FOUND]**.
