# Public reviews of car-dealership SERVICE departments, Tashkent (collected 2026-10-10)

Collected 2026-10-10, read-only, through Chrome (Yandex Maps, 2GIS; Google Maps list-level only). No reviewer names, photos or contact data recorded; quotes are short, original language + English. Year-less review dates on Yandex mean 2026.

## Method notes
- Yandex Maps (yandex.uz/maps): org page -> "Fikr-mulohazalar" (reviews) tab. Page shows at most 50 reviews per org even when the counter is higher; sorting "Oldin manfiylar" (negative first) was used to surface all non-5-star reviews. Star ratings read from the DOM.
- Yandex "ta baho" = number of ratings (stars only); "fikr-mulohaza" = number of written reviews.
- Google Maps (google.com/maps, hl=ru) used for second opinion where available.

## Patterns

1. What dominates (all rows, reviews read): price 55 > quality/repeat visits 46 > bay/time 31 > status/phone 29 > reception wait 23 > parts 21 > handover 14 > booking 8 (a review can count in several stages). Because I read mostly <=4-star reviews and sale-floor and service complaints are mixed on dealer listings, treat these as relative ordering within complaints, not as prevalence.
2. Waiting is rarely the only complaint; it is usually paired with silence. Typical sequences: promised short time (25 min) -> hours with no updates (Kia Yakkasaroy); "come, parts are ready" -> no free mechanic (BYD Yashnobod); callback promised -> never made (BYD Oloy, Megawatt, Yashnobod, Chery Rohat, Abozor). Status/phone failures (e) are as frequent as bay-time complaints (c) in BYD rows (20 vs 17).
3. Waiting-time aspect on Yandex is weakest where volume is highest: BYD Yashnobod 38% (negative majority), Kia Yakkasaroy 52%, BYD Sergeli Hadid 58%; strongest at Megawatt (89-90%). Pooled ~68% positive across 202 aspect-tagged reviews.
4. Reception wait (b) is mostly a showroom problem (no manager approaches; 10-40 min), but the same pattern appears on the service side at BYD Sergeli Hadid, BYD Megawatt (2-3 h to get a quote), BYD Yashnobod (30 min, bounced between mechanics), Abozor Mahtumkuli (reception ignores, mechanic not told the problem), plus entrance barrier/gate waits (BYD Chigatoy, Megawatt) and a paid gate (Mercedes Service).
5. Parts (d) is the most brand-specific driver of long visits: Chery (Rohat, Roodell) and Kia warranty parts "3 months / 4th month"; BYD sunroof, rear shock absorbers, parts desk unreachable. Several reviewers say the part "is available in the shop down the street" (Chery Rohat, 19 Jul 2026) or is "found for a fee" (Roodell, Jun/Jul 2025): warranty work starves while retail availability exists.
6. Handover (f) and transparency: owners not allowed in the repair zone (Megawatt, Kia Yakkasaroy), BYD Sergeli's mixed record (2023 "service done secretly", 2025 "you can watch the repair" and 2025 first-service review where five paid items were not performed, verified on cameras), cars returned dirty (Kia, Abozor, BYD Mirobod), "15% of the car not reassembled" (Mercedes Service), oil spilled/drain plug lost (Chery Rohat).
7. Brand differences: (a) Toyota group (Toyota City, Mirabad, Uzinavto, Qushbegi) - almost no waiting/status complaints on any platform; complaints are price (15 across the group, mostly Uzinavto and older) and historic competence doubts, one 2023 "no parts/no masters" review; BUT samples are tiny (Toyota City 41 reviews, Qushbegi 5, 2GIS 0-2, Google 14 at 4.4) and Yandex ratings are 5.0 everywhere, so absence of evidence is weak. (b) BYD - highest density of process complaints (b/c/e/h each about 20), with big variation by dealer: Yashnobod, Sergeli Hadid, Megawatt service site worst; Chigatoy, Mirobod, Yunusabad milder; Oloy mostly sales-floor and post-sale silence. (c) Chery (Rohat/Aster/Roodell) - parts and price. (d) Kia Yakkasaroy - waiting + appointment failures + warranty parts. (e) Multi-brand/Lada/Chevrolet service (Roodell, Avtotexxizmat, Abozor/Avtoritet) - price, quality, 1-1.5 h waits for quick jobs. (f) Premium (Mercedes Service) - tiny sample, handover quality. Ducati, Zeekr, Hyundai showroom listings: nothing about service.
8. EV/BYD-specific: (i) no unified customer database across branches ("not in our base", Urgench purchase, contract at one branch/pickup at another); (ii) some branches have no workshop and redirect (BYD Oloy, 28 May 2024) - booking mismatch; (iii) first-service (TO) upsell and unperformed items (591,000 UZS for five lines at 9,700 km; gearbox oil 1.4-1.85M UZS vs 0.5-0.7M elsewhere); (iv) early component failures cited (rear shock absorbers on Song Plus, sunroof, hybrid misfire) that drive warranty queues and parts waits; (v) accessory/bonus promises not delivered (seat covers, mud flaps, tinting voucher, SIM/telematics set-up) - a post-sale status-communication failure; (vi) new-car delivery waits of 4-8 weeks with only "ждем" (sales process, but same status-update failure); (vii) Megawatt Yandex "Repair" aspect only 42% positive despite 89% waiting-time score - fast reception, poor repair outcome.
9. Royal Motors group specifically: Toyota Tashkent City (Zargar 4): 41 Yandex reviews, 0 waiting/status complaints; Google 4.4 (14); 2GIS Toyota Royal Motors City 4.5 (17 ratings, 2 reviews). BYD Chigatoy: Yandex waiting-time 73% (n=11): negatives are a 40-min wait to be noticed then "come tomorrow" (25 Dec 2025), a 10-min wait for a consultant (23 Apr 2024), a closed barrier (30 Aug 2025); newest (1 Oct 2026) says service ticket was not controlled; Google 4.2 (28). Qushbegi (opened May 2026): only 5 reviews, no complaints about service. Ducati: no service complaints.
10. Platform effects: Yandex shows 4.7-5.0 for nearly all listings while Google shows 3.7-4.6 for the same BYD dealers, and 2GIS shows 1.2-2.8 for the Abozor/Avtoritet service sites. Negative content is available mainly by sorting "Oldin manfiylar" (negative first) on Yandex.

## Caveats

- Sample sizes are small and uneven. Yandex shows at most 50 reviews per page even when it counts more (e.g., 224 or 197); for BYD Chigatoy, BYD Oloy, BYD Sergeli Hadid and Avtotexxizmat I used the negative-first sort and read all <=4-star reviews; for the other Yandex rows I read the <=4-star reviews among the first 50 in relevance order (not a random sample). The 5-star reviews were not read for most rows (I removed them from the page before extracting text), so positive service mentions are not counted. The sort menu on some pages (BYD Yashnobod, Yunusabad, Megawatt) would not open reliably.
- "Read" counts are therefore not comparable between rows, and stage counts are counts of reviews I read, coded by me from short texts; several reviews are ambiguous between showroom and service (listings are mixed sales+service). Coding is subjective; one review can count in several stages. Column i includes neutral and positive 4-star reviews.
- Selection/platform bias: negative-first sorting over-represents complaints; Yandex star ratings of 5.0 with hundreds of ratings but only tens of written reviews suggest ratings are inflated or reviews curated; 2GIS defaults to "trusted" ordering (confirmed visits first); Google review text could not be opened (panels did not load in this automated session), so Google contributes only list-level ratings.
- Not covered or thin: Li Auto and other EV importers (no usable listing found), Lexus, BMW and Mercedes official dealers (only an independent "Mercedes-Benz Service" and an independent BMW atelier found), official Chevrolet/UzAuto Motors service centres (Yandex entries had 0-4 reviews; proxies used: Avtotexxizmat at the UzAuto/Uzinavto complex and Abozor/Avtoritet), Haval service (3 ratings), Hyundai service centres (only a showroom), independent networks (Fors.uz and Carbox on 2GIS would not render text). Independent comparators are therefore thin; Roodell and Abozor are dealer-affiliated multi-brand services.
- Dates: only Yandex reviews with dates in 2024-2026 are used for the key quotes; year-less Yandex dates are 2026. Some listings contain 2019-2023 reviews, noted where used.
- Identity of listings: Abozor Service Mahtumkuli 75/1 shares an address with BYD Yashnobod, and reviews there mention BYD service; Avtotexxizmat shares Bunyodkor 118 with Toyota Uzinavto. I treated them as separate listings and did not merge counts.
- Data hygiene: no reviewer names, photos or contact details were recorded; employee first names and phone numbers appearing in reviews were omitted. No page contained instructions addressed to an AI agent; one review ended with a social-media promotion handle, which I ignored. All access was read-only (no login, posting, voting or settings changes); on 2GIS I dismissed one informational pop-up about review sorting and did not interact with cookie banners (no decline control was offered).
- A network interruption terminated the first run mid-way; I resumed from saved context. Counts above come from the pages read in this session; no counts were estimated for unread reviews.
